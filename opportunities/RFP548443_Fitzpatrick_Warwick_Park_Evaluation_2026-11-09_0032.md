## 4. Risks and red flags

**Gaps in the documents. Submit these as questions by Nov 20:**
1. **The bid form subtotal leaves out SESC.** The formula `=SUM(E5:E17)` skips row 4 (Soil Erosion). Ask whether the evaluated total includes it. Make sure our real total includes it either way.
2. **The basketball hoop (Goalsetter MVP SS46072G3) and wall (GameTime 5045SP) are on Sheet 4 but have no line on the bid form.** That is roughly $5K–$10K+ with nowhere to put it. Ask which line it belongs in.
3. **The 5 Greenfields fitness units are on Sheet 5 but the alternate line covers only the 900 SF slab.** Ask whether we furnish and install the equipment, or the City supplies it.
4. **The Plants line is not linked to the Plant List tab.** Its total must be carried over by hand.
5. **"Playground installation" is a minimum qualification, but this scope has no playground.** Ask whether park/site-amenity experience counts (only helps if we lack playground history).
6. **The specs are reused from another job.** The headers say "Patton Park JLG Path Construction," Sheet 6 note 29 mentions a play area, and RFP §4.2 points to the affidavits in "RFP #546767." Ask the City to confirm which documents govern.
7. **Prevailing wage is not addressed.** Detroit construction contracts usually carry prevailing wage and Detroit-resident hiring requirements. Confirm before pricing labor.
8. **No required completion date or liquidated damages are stated.** The term is 2 years. We set our own duration (days from NTP), and that duration is scored.

**Cost and schedule risks:**
- **Design liability.** The contractor produces the grading and utility plans and owns the design, layout and permits (SESC, DPW ROW, possibly BSEED). Budget about $10K–$20K and 3–6 weeks of approval time.
- **Slow City process.** City Council must approve the contract, and no work starts until both the CPA **and** the SPO are issued. Realistic start is spring 2027, after the Dec 3 bid and 2–4 months of approval. That actually suits concrete and planting.
- **Payment.** Net 30 from a correct invoice under the Prompt Payment Ordinance, but City invoicing is strict. Expect slow first payments, so plan cash flow for about $100K+ of up-front material and sub costs.
- **Soil and backfill.** Only Category 1 and 2 fill is allowed, with weekly backfill tracking logs and soil tests (the City gets 2 weeks to review them). Unknown field conditions are only covered by the $10K allowance.
- **Plant warranty.** We replace anything that dies within a year, plus at least 6 waterings. Price in a replacement reserve of about 5–10%.
- **No vehicle traffic on site** (Sheet 6 note 6). Expect higher costs for moving concrete and materials by hand or with small equipment.

## 6. Recommendation

**Conditional GO.** It is a small, clear job with simple materials, one award and a scoring weight that rewards a lean local bidder. The equalization credits favor a Detroit-based small business.

**Bid as prime only if all of these are true:**
1. Hadled (or a JV partner) has **a CPSI on staff** and **5 years of park/playground project history** with 3 references, and
2. A surety will write 100% P&P bonds at about $400K, and
3. We have a concrete sub and a landscape/planting sub (5+ years each) plus a civil engineer for the grading plan.

**If #1 is not true, NO-BID as prime.** Go to the pre-bid to team up as a JV partner or win sub work from the qualified primes.
