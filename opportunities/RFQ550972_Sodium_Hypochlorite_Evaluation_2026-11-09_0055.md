## 3. Rules that apply to the product and delivery

These are not spelled out in the RFQ, but they apply to anyone who handles this product:
- **Sodium hypochlorite is a DOT hazardous material:** Class 8 corrosive, UN1791. The supplier and driver need **49 CFR hazmat training** and **shipping papers**, and loads must be secured. A typical 800–900 lb drop stays **under the 1,001 lb placard threshold**. Bigger summer loads (about 17+ five-gallon pails) would need **placards, which means a CDL driver with a hazmat endorsement.** Plan routes so each load stays under the limit, or use a carrier that has this.
- **The product must carry an EPA-registered pool sanitizer label.** Get the supplier's label and SDS. NSF/ANSI 60 certification is also good to have.
- **It loses strength over time.** 12.5% bleach weakens within weeks, faster in heat. Deliver fresh product as needed and don't stockpile. Expect the City to reject weak product.
- **Containers.** Section 2 says "5-gallon drums/pails" only, but the price sheet also has a 1-gallon line. You must check one container option (A–D) on the form: our recommendation is **D, container cost included and non-returnable**, the simplest choice.

## 5. Risks

1. **The deadline may have passed.** This is the deciding issue.
2. **The 3-year experience requirement.** A new entrant can't meet it alone.
3. **Hazmat handling and liability:** spills, chlorine-gas risk if mixed with acid, and staff injury carrying 60-lb pails inside buildings.
4. **Strict payment rules:** the City must review and approve before an invoice can be uploaded, and payment is Net 30. Expect 45–75 days from delivery to cash. You carry inventory plus about 2 months of costs (about $8K–$12K).
5. **Uneven demand:** quantities are only "estimated." The City can delete items or limit the award. Summer surges need extra truck capacity.
6. **The City can end the contract on 30 days' notice** without cause (10 days for poor performance).
7. **Odd requirements copied from a software template:** the RFQ asks for APIs and data access, which make no sense for a chemical supply. Answer "Not applicable" and offer electronic delivery logs.
